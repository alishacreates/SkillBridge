# SkillBridge — Problem Statement

**Smart India Hackathon:** SIH 2026  
**Problem Statement ID:** SIH26044  
**Title:** Portal for Academia–Industry Collaboration for Skill Mapping, Internships and Placement  
**Organization:** Ministry of Ayush — All India Institute of Ayurveda  
**Category:** Software

## 1. Background

A gap exists between the skills students acquire through academic education and the competencies industries expect from prospective employees.

Students often lack clear visibility into the technical and professional skills required for their desired career paths. Employers face challenges identifying suitable candidates, while academic institutions have limited visibility into industry requirements and opportunities for practical exposure.

Existing solutions address individual parts of this process, such as recruitment, online learning, assessments, and placement management. However, the complete cycle of skill development, industry collaboration, opportunity matching, and placement readiness is often fragmented.

## 2. Problem Definition

Develop a unified academia–industry collaboration platform that connects students, academic institutions, faculty, and industry organizations.

The platform should support skill assessment, competency mapping, identification of skill gaps, personalized learning recommendations, internships, recruitment, mentorship, industry projects, and institutional monitoring.

## 3. Proposed Solution — SkillBridge

SkillBridge is a full-stack web platform designed to connect academic capabilities with industry requirements.

Its core capabilities will include:

1. Student skill profiling and assessment.
2. Skill-gap identification and career readiness analysis.
3. Internship and job opportunity discovery.
4. Explainable student–opportunity matching.
5. Application tracking and recruitment workflows.
6. Industry learning programs, mentorship, and collaboration.
7. Faculty training and industry engagement.
8. Institutional analytics and placement monitoring.
9. Digital portfolios and evidence of student competencies.

## 4. Target Users

- Students
- Industry recruiters and organizations
- Faculty and academicians
- Educational institutions and placement coordinators
- Platform administrators

## 5. Project Objectives

- Improve visibility into industry skill requirements.
- Help students identify and address competency gaps.
- Support evidence-based opportunity matching.
- Simplify recruitment and application tracking.
- Strengthen collaboration between educational institutions and industries.
- Provide actionable insights into placement readiness and skill demand.

## 6. Scope

SkillBridge aims to address the functional requirements of SIH26044 through incremental releases.

The initial release will prioritize identity management, student profiles, skills, opportunity management, matching, and application workflows.

Later releases will introduce assessments, learning recommendations, faculty collaboration, verified portfolios, and institutional analytics.
